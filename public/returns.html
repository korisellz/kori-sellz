Return Policy

We want customers to feel confident shopping with Kori Sellz. Please review our return policy before placing an order.

Return Window

If there is an issue with your item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery.

Eligible Issues

Returns or replacements may be reviewed for damaged items, defective items, incorrect items, or missing items.

Proof Required

Photos or videos may be required so we can review the issue with the supplier.

Non-Returnable Items

Items may not be eligible for return if they were used, damaged after delivery, missing original packaging, or ordered by mistake.

Contact

For help with a return, please contact us through the Contact page with your order number and details about the issue.
