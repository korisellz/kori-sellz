Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with Kori Sellz. After your payment is confirmed, your order begins processing.

Processing Time

Orders usually process within 1-5 business days before shipment.

Estimated Delivery

Most orders are estimated to arrive within 7-15 business days after processing. Delivery times may vary depending on the product, shipping carrier, supplier processing, holidays, or unexpected delays.

Tracking

Tracking information will be available once the supplier ships your order. You can use the Track Order page to check updates when tracking becomes available.

Incorrect Address

Please make sure your shipping address is correct before completing checkout. Kori Sellz is not responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect addresses entered at checkout.

Questions

For questions about your order, please contact us through the Contact page.
