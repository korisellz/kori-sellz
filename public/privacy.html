Privacy Policy

Kori Sellz respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you shop with us.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and order details.

Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Kori Sellz does not store your full card number.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to process orders, send confirmation emails, provide tracking updates, and help with customer service.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Stripe, Resend, Render, and fulfillment suppliers to operate the store and complete orders.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact us through the Contact page.
