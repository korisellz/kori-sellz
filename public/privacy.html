Privacy Policy

Kori Sellz respects your privacy. This page explains how customer information may be collected and used.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, billing details, and order information when you place an order or contact us.

How We Use Information

We use your information to process orders, send order confirmations, provide customer support, help with tracking, and improve our store.

Payments

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Kori Sellz does not store full credit card numbers.

Sharing Information

We may share necessary order information with payment processors, shipping partners, fulfillment suppliers, and email providers to complete your order.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us through the Contact page.
